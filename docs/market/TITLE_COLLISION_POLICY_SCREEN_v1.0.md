# TITLE_COLLISION_POLICY_SCREEN_v1.0

Observed: 2026-09-27
Target title screened: `Reduce Photo Size: MB to KB`
Disposition: REJECT / DO NOT FREEZE

## FACT — Google Play collision

A live Google Play listing currently exists as:
- Title: `Reduce Photo Size - MB to KB`
- Developer: JC Apps Studio
- Visible install signal: 10K+
- Package: `ithub.compress.image.photoresizer.reducesize`

The proposed title differs only by punctuation (`:` vs `-`). This is a material buyer-facing collision.

## FACT — Adjacent title saturation

Current public Play results also include titles such as:
- `Compress Photo Size - MB to KB`
- `Photo Compressor - MB to KB`
- `Image Compressor - MB to KB`
- `Photo Size Reducer - MB to KB`

Therefore both `photo size` and `MB to KB` are high-use descriptive vocabulary in the category.

## FACT — Google Play metadata policy

Current Google Play guidance:
- app title maximum: 30 characters;
- title/metadata must accurately describe functionality;
- avoid misleading, irrelevant, excessive, or spammy metadata;
- best practices say the title should be unique and accessible and should avoid common terms.

## INFERENCE — Policy / conversion risk

`Reduce Photo Size: MB to KB` is descriptively accurate and is not inherently deceptive, but it is too close to an existing live title to be a good publication identity.

Main risks:
- weak visual/search differentiation;
- accidental association with another developer;
- lower brand memory;
- unnecessary metadata-review ambiguity;
- near-clone appearance despite an independently built product.

## Trademark / legal screen

Public web/trademark-oriented searches did not surface an obvious exact registered mark for the full phrase `Reduce Photo Size: MB to KB`.

However:
- the wording is highly descriptive of the function;
- descriptive wording generally has weak brand distinctiveness;
- absence from public search results is not formal trademark clearance;
- jurisdiction-specific registry/legal review would be required for a formal clearance opinion.

Because the Google Play title collision is already material, formal trademark clearance is not needed to reject this title for the current product.

## Replacement direction

Keep the evidence-backed keyword architecture but avoid the near-exact competitor title.

Leading candidate for the next screen:
`Reduce Photo Size: KB Limit`
Character count: 27 / 30.

Why it is promising:
- preserves `Reduce Photo Size`;
- keeps explicit KB intent;
- better matches the product's REQUIRED-limit workflow;
- does not promise exact-size success;
- current public search did not surface an exact Google Play title collision in this screening pass.

This candidate remains UNFROZEN until its own dedicated collision/policy screen completes.
